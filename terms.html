Pilot terms

Effective 14 October 2026

AfterTalk is an early pilot. Review every generated suggestion, follow-up, date, and calendar entry before relying on it.

Purpose

AfterTalk helps turn a post-conversation note into a suggested next step, a rough follow-up draft, and a calendar handoff. It is a productivity aid, not a system of record or professional adviser.

Your responsibility

  • Confirm that generated text is accurate and appropriate.
  • Confirm calendar dates, time zones, reminders, and recipients.
  • Only enter information you have the right to use.
  • Follow applicable privacy, employment, confidentiality, and recording laws.

No automatic sending

The pilot does not automatically send messages or silently write calendar events. You must review and confirm actions in the relevant calendar service.

Availability and data

The pilot is provided as available and may change or stop without notice. Browser-stored data can be lost if site data is cleared, the browser is reset, or the device is unavailable. Keep any important information in your normal calendar, CRM, or other trusted system.

No warranty

Generated outputs may be incomplete or incorrect. To the extent allowed by law, the pilot is provided without warranties, and its operators are not responsible for decisions made solely from generated content.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the product moves beyond the pilot. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised pilot terms.
