Pilot terms

Effective 21 October 2026

AfterTalk is an early pilot. AI and fallback output can be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable. Review every suggestion, follow-up, name, date, and calendar entry before using it.

Purpose

AfterTalk turns a post-conversation note into a suggested next step, a rough follow-up draft, and a calendar handoff. Depending on the pilot configuration and service availability, the result may come from a remote AI service or a deterministic rules engine in the browser. AfterTalk is a productivity aid, not a system of record or professional adviser.

Your responsibility

  • Review generated output and confirm that it is accurate, appropriate, and supported by your note.
  • Confirm names, commitments, recipients, calendar dates, time zones, and reminders.
  • Only enter information you have the right and authority to use and share with the services described in the privacy notice.
  • Follow applicable privacy, employment, confidentiality, and recording laws.
  • Keep important records in your normal calendar, CRM, or another trusted system.

AI service and fallback

When AI is enabled, requesting a card sends the current note, profile fields, and active context through the configured Supabase Edge Function to OpenAI. Service limits, safety controls, outages, or timeouts may prevent an AI result. In that case, AfterTalk uses its deterministic on-device fallback. A fallback result is not an AI result and may be more limited, but it requires the same review.

Voice transcription

Using the large microphone records a bounded voice note and sends the audio through Supabase to OpenAI for transcription. Review and correct the transcript before creating an Action Card. Do not record another person without the permission required by applicable law, policy, or agreement.

No automatic sending

AfterTalk does not automatically send messages or silently create calendar events.

  • For Google Calendar, AfterTalk opens a prefilled Google page. You must review the content and click Save there.
  • For Apple Calendar, AfterTalk downloads an `.ics` file. You must open it and confirm Add.

Availability and local data

The pilot is provided as available and may change, be rate-limited, or stop without notice. Profile information, context history, preferences, and prepared actions are stored in the browser and can be lost if site data is cleared, the browser is reset, or the device is unavailable. There is no AfterTalk account or cross-device sync in this pilot.

Acceptable use

Do not use AfterTalk to submit unlawful content, credentials, payment information, highly sensitive personal data, or confidential information you are not authorized to process. Do not try to bypass access controls, origin restrictions, rate limits, or other safeguards.

No warranty

Generated output may omit context, infer the wrong action, or choose an unsuitable date. To the extent allowed by law, the pilot is provided without warranties, and its operators are not responsible for decisions made solely from generated content.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the product changes. The effective date will be updated with revised terms. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised pilot terms.
