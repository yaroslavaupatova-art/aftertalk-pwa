Privacy notice

Pilot version · Effective 14 October 2026

Short version: AfterTalk currently works locally in your browser. It has no user accounts, analytics, advertising trackers, or application database.

Information you enter

You may enter profile information, temporary context, conversation notes, next steps, and follow-up drafts. The pilot stores this information in your browser’s local storage on the device you are using.

Voice input

When browser speech recognition is available and you choose to use it, your browser or operating-system provider may process speech under its own privacy terms. AfterTalk does not store an audio recording. You can always use keyboard dictation or type instead.

Calendar handoff

  • Google Calendar: AfterTalk opens a prefilled Google Calendar page. The information is shared with Google only when you choose this option, and you must confirm saving it.
  • Apple Calendar: AfterTalk creates a calendar file on your device. You decide whether to add it to your calendar.

Storage and deletion

There is no cloud sync in this pilot. Clearing this site’s browser data deletes the profile and history stored by AfterTalk on that browser. Data does not automatically transfer between devices.

What not to enter

Do not enter passwords, payment information, highly sensitive personal data, or confidential information you are not authorized to process. Capture your own post-conversation notes rather than recording other people without permission.

Changes

This notice may change as accounts, secure cloud storage, production AI, or additional integrations are introduced. The effective date will be updated when the data practices change.

Questions

For this private pilot, contact the person who shared the AfterTalk link with you.
